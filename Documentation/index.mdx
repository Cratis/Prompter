Prompter helps you look up answers about Cratis on the community Discord. Ask about the product and task
you are working on, then open the cited sources to check the reply. If it does not answer your question,
you can narrow the question or ask the community for help. [Ask your first question →](/prompter/getting-started/)
</TopicHero>

```text
you · #help
  How do I define a command in Arc?

🎭 Prompter · replies to you
  In Arc a command is a record marked [Command]; its Handle() method
  returns the event(s) that happened...

  Sources: cratis.io/arc/commands
```

Start with one question about what you are trying to do. Naming the Cratis product, feature or error gives
Prompter something specific to look up and gives other community members context if you need their help.
A cited reply is a starting point: check that the linked page supports the part you intend to use before
applying it to your project.

## What it doesn't do

Prompter is not a general-purpose chatbot or a substitute for checking a technical answer. It uses indexed
Cratis material, including documentation and other configured sources, rather than treating a plausible
reply as evidence. When it cannot find grounding, it can refuse; that does not establish that the feature
you asked about does not exist.

Where Prompter participates depends on the server configuration: some channels require a mention or a
command, while configured ask channels and forums can receive automatic replies. If you need a person's
judgment, ask the community rather than waiting for the bot to resolve the question for you.

:::tip[Honesty is the feature]
Open the cited page and compare it with the claim you plan to use. A source link gives you a way to check
an answer, not a guarantee that the answer is correct. If the page does not support the claim, say what
does not match and ask for help before relying on it.
:::
